Two-Body Gravity Simulator

Two masses orbit their common centre of mass (COM) under mutual gravity F = G m1m2/r².

Both bodies are integrated directly from Newton’s law of gravitation (velocity-Verlet). The simulation starts in the COM frame with zero total momentum, so the red COM marker stays fixed while the bodies move about it. Changing a mass restarts the orbit with the same semi-major axis; the heavier body sits closer to the COM, with m1r1 = m2r2. The period follows T² = 4π²a³/(G(m1+m2)). Units are arbitrary.
